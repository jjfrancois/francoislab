Sofia Cardenas Caraballo is a junior Biology major at Boise State University. As a member of the François Lab, she is investigating how p53 status affects three-dimensional (3D) cancer cell migration post-DNA damage. 
Specifically, she is developing novel 3D cancer models and assays to better understand p53’s role in cancer cell migration and its potential implications for cancer progression following DNA-damaging treatments. 
She plans to pursue a career in oncology, where she hopes to apply what she learns through her project to help improve care for future patients.
