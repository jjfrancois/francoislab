Alena Gonzalez is a senior Biology major at Boise State University. As a member of the François Lab, she is investigating how key components of the DNA damage response pathway affect cancer cell migration in three-dimensional environments. Through her research, she is gaining experience integrating proteomics data with live-cell imaging results. Her work linking molecular changes to cell behavior has strengthened her interest in using sequencing-based approaches to address biomedical problems. Ultimately, she hopes to pursue a career as a physician, using her research experience to better understand the processes that contribute to cancer metastasis and connect scientific discoveries with patient care.
